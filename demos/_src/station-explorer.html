The five stops of a backup

Click a stop or focus it with Tab; the panel below describes that stop.

The top row captures and protects the data; the bottom row keeps it until it expires and tests it regularly. The ghost box is a stage that isn't built yet, and it can't be clicked.

INTAKECapture and protect

archive · planned

In one sentenceThe nightly snapshot is encrypted, copied to a second region, kept for 35 days and tested every month with a real restore.

Snapshot. A disk-level consistent copy of the database. It doesn't stop writes; there may be an I/O pause of a few seconds.
Encryption. The snapshot is encrypted with the key in the vault. When the key rotates, old snapshots keep being read with the old version.
Copy. The encrypted snapshot is sent to the second region; if a region is lost, the restore runs from here.
Retention. Snapshots are kept for 35 days. While the delete lock is on, an unexpired snapshot can't be deleted even by hand.
Test. Every month a random snapshot is restored to a temporary copy and check queries are run. An untested backup doesn't count as a backup.

Accessibility

Stops are visited in Tab order; focus opens the preview, Enter or Space selects. A screen reader reads the button as “pressed / not pressed” and, thanks to aria-live, announces the new text when the panel changes. On narrow screens (below 720px) the SVG is hidden and the stops stack; that is why the sentence that explains the arrows must also be in the panel or the summary.
