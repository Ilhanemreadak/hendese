The middle zone is proportional: 10 units = 4 ms, with a ruler mark every 40 ms. The side zones are not proportional and are set apart with .dim brackets; the arrow between them shows the process moving on to the next zone. If one phase has two possible faults, the second lamp is stacked above the first (4 and 5).

A lamp is a circle and a number inside <g class="lamp">; a lit lamp gets data-state="on". Its color means fault by default and turns to warning with .s-warn. A lamp that cannot be measured is drawn dashed and faded with data-state="ghost"; a lamp under maintenance gets .ln-dash on its circle. A lit phase is drawn with class="ln-strong ln-c fill-soft s-bad": a single .s-* class sets both the line and the fill color. In the list, the same row is darkened with <li data-state="on"> and starts with “Lit ·”. The leader and phase tied to a lit lamp are drawn in red too; a lamp in warning colors only itself. Several lamps can be lit on one panel at once, but lit rows do not move to the top of the list; they stay at their own number: the number order is the order of positions in the drawing.

Accessibility

The lamp number lives inside the SVG; a screen reader reads only the list. So a lit row is marked with a word in the list (“Lit ·”), and color is never left on its own. The list must be an <ol>: the item number is the lamp's number.
