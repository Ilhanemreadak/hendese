A shape with no fill class is transparent; the line behind it shows through. With overlapping boxes, giving the front one .fill-paper is the only way to hide the lines behind it. Draw arrowheads either as lines (l-8-4M… l-8 4) or as a .fill-draw triangle; do not mix the two in the same figure.
