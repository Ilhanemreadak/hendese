Hendese · Components

Survey logNumbered notes, entries with a header.

.survey-log is an ordered notebook: each entry has its number and header on the left and labeled paragraphs on the right. For texts that answer “when, where, what did we see, what did we do” in order, like a post-incident timeline, a change log or a measurement record.

Classes
.survey-log · .sl-meta · .sl-no · .sl-body · .sl-label
Status
li.s-ok · li.s-warn · li.s-bad colors the number
Structure
<li> inside an <ol>; header as a <dl>
Narrow column
If the container is narrower than 720px, the header moves above the text
JS
Not needed

01

Anatomy

A 200px header column on the left, text at a readable width on the right. A heavy rule on top opens the log, and a thin rule under each entry closes it.

Example · a single entry
  1. L-07
    Date
    2026-10-05
    Environment
    staging
    Author
    platform
    Observation

    After the cache restarted, database read load tripled for the first 10 minutes.

    Decision

    The top 1,000 keys will be warmed before a restart; the script was added to the cache-warm job.

.sl-no
The entry number. The list order already comes from the <ol>; the number lets readers refer to it from elsewhere (“see L-07”).
.sl-meta dl
The header: short fields like date, environment and source. A long value wraps within the line.
.sl-label
The kind of paragraph. Using the same label order in every entry makes the log scannable.

02

Post-incident timeline

What was seen and what was done, in time order. Time and source in the header; observation and action under separate labels in the text.

Example · payment queue backlog
  1. T-01
    Time
    09:41
    Source
    alarm
    Observation

    billing-worker queue depth went from 120 to 4,800 in 5 minutes; all workers are up.

  2. T-02
    Time
    09:48
    Source
    log
    Observation

    In every job, the call to the payment provider times out after 30 seconds.

    Action

    Checked the provider's status page; no known outage.

  3. T-03
    Time
    10:02
    Source
    network
    Observation

    The NAT gateway has hit its connection limit; new outbound connections are being rejected.

    Action

    Opened a second gateway and split the worker subnet's routing in two.

  4. T-04
    Time
    10:31
    Source
    dashboard
    Outcome

    The queue drained in 29 minutes. No jobs lost; 212 payments were processed late.

03

Change log and measurement record

The same log holds two different kinds of record. A change labels impact and rollback; a measurement labels value and interpretation.

Example · infrastructure change log
  1. C-41
    Date
    2026-09-29
    Version
    infra v318
    Author
    network team
    Change

    The internal DNS zone's default TTL dropped from 300 to 60 seconds.

    Impact

    Resolver query count rises about fivefold; the cost difference is under $3 a month.

    Rollback

    Reapply the same change with ttl = 300.

  2. C-42
    Date
    2026-10-01
    Version
    infra v319
    Author
    platform
    Change

    Build workers moved to spot instances.

    Impact

    Build cost dropped 58%; on a reclaimed instance the job starts over.

Example · latency measurements
  1. M-01
    Environment
    prod
    Tool
    k6 · 200 VU
    Duration
    10 min
    Measurement

    GET /v1/invoices · p50 41 ms, p95 182 ms, p99 390 ms · errors 0.02%

    Interpretation

    p99 comes from cache-miss requests; within the 400 ms target.

  2. M-02
    Environment
    prod
    Tool
    k6 · 400 VU
    Duration
    10 min
    Measurement

    p50 58 ms, p95 410 ms, p99 1,240 ms · errors 0.9%

    Interpretation

    The connection pool saturates at 50; the pool will be raised to 100 and the measurement repeated (see M-03).

04

States

Give an entry .s-* and its number takes that color; the rules and text do not change. Also state the status in a header field: color only speeds up scanning.

Example · rollback attempts
  1. R-01
    Time
    14:05
    Outcome
    failed
    Action

    Rolled web-api back to 1.8.2; the new replicas could not pull the image.

  2. R-02
    Time
    14:19
    Outcome
    partial
    Action

    Fixed the image registry permission; 2 of 3 replicas came up.

  3. R-03
    Time
    14:31
    Outcome
    succeeded
    Action

    Moved the last replica to a new node; the 5xx rate dropped to 0.1%.

  4. R-04
    Time
    15:00
    Outcome
    —none
    Note

    An entry with no class stays in the drawing color: an observation or a note, a record with no outcome.

The accepted classes are .s-ok, .s-warn, .s-bad, .s-sec and .s-neutral; only the .sl-no color changes. Do not leave an empty value blank: show —, plus a .visually-hidden word for screen readers. An empty dd is skipped, and the field seems to be missing.

05

Dark scene

The log looks the same on a dark panel; the rules and header colors resolve according to the container's theme.

Example · on-call log, dark panel

On-call log · night

  1. N-01
    Time
    02:14
    Alarm
    disk-usage
    Observation

    The logs-db disk is 91% full; growing 1% an hour.

    Action

    Moved partitions older than 30 days to the archive; usage dropped to 64%. Handed over to the morning team.

06

Do / don't

Keep observation and interpretation apart: what was seen under one label, what was thought or done under another.

Do · separate labels
  1. N-12
    Time
    14:05
    Observation

    5xx rate at 4%.

    Action

    Rolled back the last release.

Don't · everything in one paragraph
  1. N-12
    Time
    14:05

    Errors went up, probably the release, we rolled back and then it kind of got better.

Accessibility

The <ol> gives the screen reader the number and order of entries; the <dl> in the header pairs field names with their values. .sl-label is only a visual heading and is not part of the heading outline; keep the label text short and meaningful on its own.
