Fault panelWhich phase, which lamp is lit.

A fault panel shows at a glance where a process or a group of services broke: the phases in a strip, the possible faults on numbered lamps. Each lamp is a row in the list below; the list is the text equivalent of the drawing and is always written.

01

Anatomy

Zone brackets on top, the phase strip in the middle, lamps above the strip and a time ruler below it. The middle zone is proportional to time; the side zones are not.

  1. DNS did not resolve: no record, or the resolver is unreachable.
  2. TLS handshake failed: the certificate expired or the chain is incomplete.
  3. web-api returned 5xx: application error, stack trace in the log.
  4. Lit · The database phase took 120 ms (budget 30 ms): an unindexed query on the orders table.
  5. Connection pool exhausted: requests queued while waiting for the database.
  6. Client timeout: the response did not complete within 2 seconds.

The middle zone is proportional: 10 units = 4 ms, with a ruler mark every 40 ms. The side zones are not proportional and are set apart with .dim brackets; the arrow between them shows the process moving on to the next zone. If one phase has two possible faults, the second lamp is stacked above the first (4 and 5).

02

Lamp states

A lamp can be off, lit or in warning. A lamp under maintenance is dashed; a lamp that cannot be measured is drawn as a ghost. The number stays readable in every state.

Example · five states

maintenance

ghost

A lamp is a circle and a number inside <g class="lamp">; a lit lamp gets data-state="on". Its color means fault by default and turns to warning with .s-warn. A lamp that cannot be measured is drawn dashed and faded with data-state="ghost"; a lamp under maintenance gets .ln-dash on its circle. A lit phase is drawn with class="ln-strong ln-c fill-soft s-bad": a single .s-* class sets both the line and the fill color. In the list, the same row is darkened with <li data-state="on"> and starts with “Lit ·”. The leader and phase tied to a lit lamp are drawn in red too; a lamp in warning colors only itself. Several lamps can be lit on one panel at once, but lit rows do not move to the top of the list; they stay at their own number: the number order is the order of positions in the drawing.

04

Do / don't

A lamp is a number that points to a list. Colored dots with no number and no list are not a panel; they are a puzzle.

Do · number and list
  1. The build did not start.
  2. Lit · Tests failed: 3 unit tests.
Don't · color only
Accessibility

The lamp number lives inside the SVG; a screen reader reads only the list. So a lit row is marked with a word in the list (“Lit ·”), and color is never left on its own. The list must be an <ol>: the item number is the lamp's number.
