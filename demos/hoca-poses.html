Intro

Hendese · Hoca

Hoca · posesCharacter, 21 poses, speech bubble.

Hoca is an orange, pixel block character. It is painted with canvas and has no image file. Its teacher role comes from the glasses, the item in hand and the pose; it speaks one short sentence that repeats what the text already says.

01

Character

A flat block body, two eye slits, side nubs and four legs. Its dimensions are fixed; Hoca does not scale.

Grid
44 × 36 cells, each cell 2 × 2 px; 88 × 72 px on screen. No scaling, sharp edges (image-rendering: pixelated).
Anchor point
The bottom center of the feet ({x: 44, y: 68}); in the sit pose, the seat point ({x: 44, y: 60}). The red cross on the left marks this point.
Personality
Small glasses, the item in hand, the pose. Facial expression comes from the eye slits alone.
Painting
Each pose is drawn to a canvas once and turned into a data URL; no external file is loaded.
Accessibility
The layer and all copies are aria-hidden; everything Hoca says is also written in the text.

The engine creates its own Hoca instance inside Hendese.start() (when the rail has #hoca-home or a scene carries still). The gallery and playground on this page open separate instances with Hendese.createHoca() and put copies inside the boxes with place().

04

Speech bubble rules

A speech bubble is one short sentence. It repeats something the text already says, and it appears once per scene, at the key moment.

  • Short. At most 230px wide; a sentence longer than two lines doesn't fit the bubble and weighs on the reader.
  • No new information. The bubble never reaches a screen reader and is hidden on narrow screens; everything it says must be written on the page.
  • Position. The bubble sits to Hoca's left. In render() it moves to the right when there is no room on the left; place() doesn't do that on its own, so put Hoca where the bubble has room. bubbleUp lifts the bubble above head level.
  • Font. If the text has a character Pixelify Sans doesn't cover (for example ≠), the whole bubble falls back to the mono font.
Do · one sentence, already in the text

The result line says “Backup verified: checksum matches”; the bubble shortens it.

Don't · a paragraph, new information

A long instruction that appears nowhere on the page: it is lost on narrow screens, in print and to screen readers.

Example · mono fallback and top bubble

On the left, a bubble containing “≠” fell back to the mono font. On the right, bubbleUp: true: the bubble sits above the head, with its tail at the bottom corner.

05

With motion off and on narrow screens

Hoca is visible only on screens 1100px and wider. Reduced motion stops it but doesn't remove it.

StateHoca
Live (≥ 1100px)On the fixed layer; the engine moves it from scene to scene with render().
Reduced motion (desktop)A still copy in each scene's still pose, placed with place() like the gallery on this page.
< 1100pxThe layer and all copies are hidden (display: none !important). Only the pose names remain in the gallery.
PrintHidden.
No JSAbsent; Hoca is painted entirely with JS.
Bubble transition

The bubble appears with a short two-step (steps(2)) transition; with reduced motion there is no transition.

06

API

createHoca() returns a standalone sprite instance. The engine manages the Hoca in scenes; you don't call it directly.

MemberWhat it does
Hendese.createHoca()Paints every pose, adds a fixed layer (.hoca-layer) to body and returns the instance. Each call means a new layer and a new paint; one or two instances per page are enough.
render(s)Draws on the fixed layer. s: {visible, x, y (viewport px), pose, face (1 | -1), squash (0..1), bubble, bubbleUp, tag, alpha}.
place(container, o)Puts a still copy inside the container; the container must be positioned (position: relative). o: {left, top (CSS length), pose, face, bubble, bubbleUp, tag}.
clearPlaced()Removes every copy this instance has placed.
size{w: 88, h: 72, scale: 2}.
anchorsPose name → anchor point (px): {x: 44, y: 68}, {x: 44, y: 60} for sit.
Classes.hoca, .hoca-img, .hoca-bubble (.on, .is-up, .is-right, .mono), .hoca-tag, .hoca-still.
