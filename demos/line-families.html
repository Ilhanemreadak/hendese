Line familiesWeight is a hierarchy, pattern is a meaning.

Every line in a Hendese drawing says something. The weight family (guide, drawing, emphasis, dimension) tells what is in front; the pattern family (solid, dashed, ghost, blocked) tells what is real. Status colors add only color on top of these two.

Weight
.ln-pencil · .pencil · .pencil-2 · .ln-draw · .ln-strong · .dim
Meaning
.ln-dash · .ln-ghost · .ln-block · .ln-ok · .ln-warn · .ln-bad
Fill
.fill-paper · .fill-scene · .fill-draw

01

Weight family

Four weights and a dimension line. A drawing's body is drawn with ln-draw; guides step back, and emphasis goes to one thing only.

Example · legend sheet · weight

.pencil and .pencil-2 are aliases from the fixed .sheet grid: .pencil and .ln-pencil draw the same line. Prefer the .ln- prefix in new drawings; .pencil-2 is the only option for the grid.

02

Pattern and status

A solid line shows what is real; a dashed line shows a template or a boundary. A ghost is a part that does not exist yet or no longer exists; a blocked path always ends in a stop mark.

Example · legend sheet · meaning

03

Fills

There are three fills: paper for the face in front, scene ground for faces at the back or side, and drawing color for small marks. SVG defaults to no fill.

Example · paper, scene, drawing

.fill-paper
face in front

.fill-scene
back and side faces

.fill-draw
dot, square, arrowhead

together: isometric box

A shape with no fill class is transparent; the line behind it shows through. With overlapping boxes, giving the front one .fill-paper is the only way to hide the lines behind it. Draw arrowheads either as lines (l-8-4M… l-8 4) or as a .fill-draw triangle; do not mix the two in the same figure.

Accessibility

With color blindness, red and green lines can look the same. So a blocked path is drawn with both a broken pattern and a stop mark, with a .state word next to it. Because the SVG is aria-hidden="true", the same information must also be in the figure caption.
