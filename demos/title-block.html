Title blockThe sheet's credits.

.titleblock is the information table in the corner of a technical drawing: it says at a glance what a figure or page shows, in which environment, at what scale and when. The markup is a <dl>; the first row, .tb-head, carries the sheet's name.

Classes
.titleblock · .tb-head
Structure
<div><dt><dd> inside a <dl>
Width
380px, never overflows its container; full width if the content column is narrower than 720px
State
No status color of its own; written in a row with .state.s-*
JS
Not needed

Each row is a field: a 132px label on the left, the value on the right. Thin pencil-colored lines separate the rows; the first row's value is bold.

05

States

The title block has no status color or dashed draft look of its own. The sheet's status is written in a row with a .state label; one rule each covers empty and long values.

Say the status with the word in the row, not with the title block's color; .state is printed with 1px units outside the drawing. Do not delete an empty field row: “—” is visible, screen readers hear the .visually-hidden text, and the reader knows the field was not forgotten. By the language's rules a draft sheet should be drawn with a dashed frame; the title block does not support this yet. Do not fake it with inline styles.

Do / don't

Accessibility

<div> wrappers inside a <dl> are valid HTML; screen readers read each field with its name and value. The title block must stay outside the figure's aria-hidden drawing: it is part of the figure's text equivalent.
