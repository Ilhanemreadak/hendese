SheetThe frame of a drawing: header, side column, map, caption.

Every figure sits on a sheet. .frame is a four-area grid; for figures that need no side column there is the lighter .fig-sheet, and for a fixed 1000 × 600 unit grid there is .sheet.

Classes
.frame · .frame-bg · .f-head · .f-side · .f-map · .f-foot · .fig-sheet · .fig-cap · .sheet
Breakpoint
One column when the column (col container) drops below 760px, narrow padding below 720px
JS
Not needed

01

Anatomy

A sheet is made of four areas and a background layer. Header on top, side column on the right, drawing in the middle, caption at the bottom.

Example · the skeleton of the four areas

f-head · chip

f-head · figure title

f-side · side column. A step list, prose or a title block goes here; its width is the --side-w token.

It spans three rows, from the header down to the footer.

f-map · drawing area

f-foot. Caption, one-line summary or station panel.

.frame-bg is an empty layer that draws the background and border; it is always the first child and carries aria-hidden="true". The areas place themselves on the grid, so their order is free. Below, the same skeleton is filled with a real drawing.

02

Variants

frame if you need a side column, fig-sheet for a single figure in the flow, sheet for a map with a fixed ratio.

.fig-sheet carries the same background but is a single column; the chip, the .fig-cap title, the drawing and the figcaption.figcap flow one under another. Write it as a <figure>.

Example · fig-sheet · backup chain

.sheet is a fixed 1000 × 600 unit grid. The .sl labels and .station buttons inside it assume this size when --aw / --ah are not given. Below 720 pixels the SVG is hidden and the labels stack, so everything that carries meaning must also be written in a .deck-cap or a station.

04

Sizes and breakpoints

A sheet has few sizes of its own, and all of them are tokens. Breakpoints follow the column the sheet sits in, not the screen; only .sheet looks at the screen.

ValueWhereWhat it does
--side-w.frameSide column width: clamp(230px, 20vw, 290px).
--sp-8 --sp-9.framePadding 20px / 28px; column gap --sp-10 (32px), row gap --sp-6 (14px).
--r-lg.frame-bgCorner radius of the background; background color --scene-bg, border --line.
--sp-7 --sp-8 --sp-6.fig-sheetPadding of the single-column sheet (16px 20px 14px); --sp-5 --sp-4 in a narrow column.
1000 × 600.sheetFixed unit grid; grows to at most 1100px.
@container col (max-width: 760px).frameThe side column drops below the drawing and the title block aligns left.
@container col (max-width: 719px).frame, .fig-sheetPadding narrows, the footer becomes one column, the title block goes full width.
@media (max-width: 719px).sheetWhen the screen narrows, .sheet turns into a list; this is the one window-based exception.
Breakpoints follow the column

.frame switches to one column based on the width of the nearest col container. By default that container is main and .sticky-text; a sheet in a narrow column drops its side column below even on a wide screen. If you set up your own narrow column (a card, a side panel, a two-up grid), give it container: col / inline-size; in a narrow column with no container, the frame looks at the nearest wide container and keeps its side column.

Do / don't

A sheet gives a figure an identity: a number, a title and a one-sentence description. The frame alone says nothing.

Accessibility

The drawing's SVG and .frame-bg carry aria-hidden="true"; the figure's meaning must also be in the .beats list, the .oneliner summary or the .figcap caption. Write .fig-sheet as a <figure> and its caption as a <figcaption>; a screen reader reads them together.
