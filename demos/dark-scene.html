Hendese · Blueprint

Dark sceneDraw a container dark or light, regardless of the theme.

.scene-dark switches a container's color-scheme to dark. Because the tokens are defined with light-dark(), every line, fill and component inside resolves to dark values, even in the light theme. Use it to show a place that is different in context, like “inside the network” or “the night shift”. .scene-light does the opposite: it draws a container light in the dark theme.

01

How it works

The class does three things: it switches the container's color scheme, reads the text color again and sets the background. fig-sheet and frame draw their background with their own frame-bg layer; the class does not override it.

Example · same markup, two scenes

.scene-light

billing-worker drained the queue.

Idle

.scene-dark

billing-worker drained the queue.

Idle

The markup of the two boxes differs only in the class name, and neither has an inline background. Whatever the page theme, the left box is drawn light and the right one dark: --scene-bg, --ink, --ok and the button's surface tokens resolve according to the scene's scheme.

Automatic background

A bare div.scene-dark gets its background on its own: if its direct child is not a .frame-bg, the container gets background: var(--scene-bg). Do not write an inline background. A scene that needs corners and edges carries a .frame-bg layer; that layer then draws the background.

Drawings

Line and fill classes read tokens, so a drawing works unchanged in a dark scene. The paper fill turns into a dark surface and the drawing color into light blue.

03

Light scene

scene-light does the opposite: on a dark-themed page it draws a container light. Use it to show a print preview, a paper printout or a light-background screen.

Example · print preview · light sheet

Scenes can be nested: the nearest class wins. A .scene-light box inside a dark sheet resolves light, and a .scene-dark inside a light scene resolves dark. Both classes supply the background themselves when there is no .frame-bg layer of their own.

In context

A dark scene points to a place: inside the network, the night shift, the production environment. The typical use is to make a whole sheet dark.

Do / don't

In a dark scene, colors must come from tokens. A fixed color was picked for the light theme; on a dark background it either disappears or shouts.

Accessibility

The dark tokens are chosen to give at least AA contrast on a dark background; this holds when the page is in the light theme too. Use scenes only for context: overriding the reader's chosen theme on long text with .scene-dark or .scene-light ignores their preference.
