Rail

.nav-page[aria-current="page"] sets the page you are on in bold. The .nav-link number sits inside .num and lines up with tabular figures.

.is-active and aria-current="true" are not written by hand: Hendese.init() finds the .nav-link[href="#…"] links inside #rail and marks the active one as you scroll. This example is outside #rail, so it stays still.

In live mode, at the top of the page the rail turns .compact: the labels hide and only the numbers remain; they come back on hover or focus.

05

Narrow space

Below 1100px the rail becomes a drawer, and the fixed rail gives way to the top bar. The rulers and Hoca's home are hidden; the content becomes a single column.

Example · top bar (visible below 1100px)
Platform handbook 03 Retries

So that the bar shows in the example, display:flex;position:static is set inline and the ids are removed. In the real bar, #nav-open carries aria-controls="rail" and aria-expanded; while the drawer is open, focus stays inside the rail, Esc or a click on the scrim closes it, and focus returns to the button.

Why 1100px

The threshold is written in two places, as a media query in CSS and as matchMedia('(min-width:1100px)') in JS, and it is a contract, not a setting. Media queries cannot read variables, so there is no token.

06

Column width

Components look at the column they sit in, not at the window. The main element is a container named col; tables, fact lists, steps and sheets read their breakpoints from that container's width.

main carries container: col / inline-size. When a component is placed in a narrow column (a side panel, half of a two-column layout, the text column of a sticky scene), it switches to its narrow layout even if the window is wide. To make your own narrow column a container, give it the same name; the nearest col container wins.

07

Full skeleton

The smallest complete page to copy and fill in. This section is quiet: Hoca stays home in the rail and the rail fades, because you should not be distracted while reading long code.

To make a section quiet, <article data-hoca="quiet"> is enough. While the section is active, the rail gets .hoca-quiet and Hoca's home turns semi-transparent; scenes do not call for Hoca.

Is it enough

This skeleton reads without JS too: the rail stays a static table of contents and the sections stay plain text. Hendese.init() only adds the drawer, section tracking, the reading bar and the copy buttons.
