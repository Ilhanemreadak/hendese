Hoca
Intro

Hendese · Mascot

HocaA teacher who walks beside the story, not one who carries it.

An orange, pixel-block character. Painted with canvas; there is no image file. Scenes call Hoca in; when idle, Hoca waits at the home position on the rail.

01

Character

A flat block body, two eye slits, side nubs and four legs. The teacher personality comes only from the glasses, the props and the poses.

  • The speech bubble is one short sentence that repeats something the text already says; it never carries new information.
  • Hoca speaks once per scene, at the key moment.
  • Hoca never stands on top of an important label; the scene leaves room.
  • Hidden from screen readers (aria-hidden); a purely visual companion.

02

Poses

21 poses. The dashed line is the ground; the anchor point is the bottom center of the feet (the seat point for sit).

Poses are visible only on screens 1100px and wider (hidden in print and on narrow screens).

04

Behavior rules

Each frame, the controller decides who owns Hoca; scenes only request.

StateWhat happens
No scene requests HocaHoca stands idle at the home position, #hoca-home on the rail. Dimmed (45%) if the active section has data-hoca="quiet".
Several scenes request HocaThe first scene in registration order wins.
Between two nearby points (≤ 260px)Walks for 650 ms (walkA/walkB); bubble and tag are hidden while walking.
To or from home, long distance, part navigation320 ms: fades out at the old spot and appears at the new one; never crosses unrelated content.
Reduced motion (desktop)A still copy in the still pose in every scene; Hendese.refresh() places them again.
< 1100px, printHidden.
Walking
When walking Hoca inside a scene, tie the pose to position: Math.floor(x / 16) % 2 ? 'walkA' : 'walkB', only while x changes; face is the direction of travel. When carrying a crate, use crateWalkA/B.

05

API

MemberWhat it does
Hendese.createHoca()A new sprite instance; paints every pose and adds a fixed layer. The engine already creates its own instance; call this only for standalone use (e.g. the gallery on this page).
render(s)Draws on the fixed layer. s: {visible, x, y (viewport px), pose, face, squash (0..1), bubble, bubbleUp, tag, alpha}.
place(container, o)Puts a still copy inside a container (the container needs position:relative). o: {left, top (CSS length), pose, face, bubble, bubbleUp, tag}.
clearPlaced()Removes all still copies.
size, anchors{w:88, h:72, scale:2}; anchor points of the poses (px).
Scene requestA scene's render returns hoca: {x, y, pose, face, bubble, bubbleUp, tag, squash, hidden}; coordinates are in drawing units.

The bubble sits on the left; if there is no room on the left (x < bubble width + 20), it moves to the right. Text with characters outside Pixelify Sans (e.g. ≠) falls back to the mono font.
